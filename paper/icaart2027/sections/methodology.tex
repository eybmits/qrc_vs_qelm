\subsection{Numerical Evaluation}
\label{sec:methods}

\subsubsection{The Common-Suffix Diagnostic}
\label{app:common-suffix-diagnostic}

For suffix $s$ and prefix branch $k$, let $y_{sk}\in\mathbb R^m$ be the
output, $\bar y_s$ its branch mean, and $\widehat y_s$ the shared prediction.
Equal-weight empirical averages and the Euclidean norm give
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 &\frac1m\mathbb E_{s,k}\|y_{sk}-\widehat y_s\|_2^2\\
 &\quad=\frac1m\mathbb E_{s,k}\|y_{sk}-\bar y_s\|_2^2\\
 &\qquad+\frac1m\mathbb E_s\|\bar y_s-\widehat y_s\|_2^2 .
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:panel-decomposition}
\end{equation}
The cross term vanishes. The residual combines feature approximation,
finite-data estimation, ridge regularization, and suffix-mean sampling
variability; it does not isolate feature approximation.

For $n_{\rm pre}$ conditionally independent prefixes, the history numerator
averages $(n_{\rm pre}-1)/n_{\rm pre}$ times population conditional variance.
The registered four-prefix correction $4/3$ preserves all classifications
and reported effects. Equation~\eqref{eq:panel-decomposition} uses uncorrected
terms. Neither it nor the correction estimates $D_d$ or $r_{\rm vis}$.
For the exact benchmark, reachable-closure populations 0 and 1 with
probabilities $1-p,p$ give conditional standard deviation
$D_d\sqrt{p(1-p)}$, while the minimax error remains $D_d/2$.
At $p=0.01$, four independent draws have zero variance with probability
$0.99^4+0.01^4\simeq96.06\%$ despite the unchanged worst-case floor.

\subsubsection{Models and Evaluation Protocol}

Targets (subscript $T$) have $N_T\in\{2,3,4\}$ qubits and local-loss rate
$\gamma_T$. Each fixed input $a$ drives
\begin{align}
 H_T(a)&=\sum_{i<j}J_{ij}X_iX_j+0.5\sum_i Z_i+a\sum_iX_i,\nonumber\\
 \dot\rho&=-i[H_T(a),\rho]+\gamma_T\sum_i\mathcal D[\sigma_i^-]\rho,
 \label{eq:target-qrc-model}
\end{align}
Here $\dot\rho=d\rho/d\tau$ uses physical time $\tau$, while $t$ indexes
discrete inputs. The dissipator is
$\mathcal D[L]\rho=L\rho L^\dagger-\tfrac12\{L^\dagger L,\rho\}$;
$\dagger$, $[\cdot,\cdot]$, and $\{\cdot,\cdot\}$ denote Hermitian adjoint,
commutator, and anticommutator. Couplings are uniform on $[-1,1]$,
$\Delta t=0.5$, and inputs use a uniform 33-point alphabet on $[0,1]$.
Single-site and same-axis two-site Pauli expectations give
9, 18, and 30 outputs, respectively.

The fixed six-qubit QELM resets and encodes the newest $d\leq6$ inputs as
$\sqrt{1-a}|0\rangle+\sqrt a|1\rangle$, leaving unused qubits in $|0\rangle$.
Independent uniform $[-1,1]$ $XX$ couplings and transverse field $0.5$
evolve the state for $\tau=0.5$ without dissipation.
Single-site $X,Y,Z$ expectations at one, two, or four
nested checkpoints give $B\in\{18,36,72\}$ observable--checkpoint features.

\emph{Cohorts and held-out data.}
The confirmatory cohort uses $N_T\in\{2,3\}$,
$\gamma_T\in\{0,0.2,0.5,1,2,4\}$, and $d\in\{1,2,4,6\}$.
The dense map adds $d=3,5$ and rates $0.75,1.25,1.5,2.5,3$ with
12 roots and 108 seeds disjoint from the original experiment.
The prospectively frozen four-qubit follow-up, designed after map inspection,
uses 12 new roots, 96 disjoint seeds, and otherwise unchanged settings.
Checks cover negative history decay, non-increasing frontiers, and the
$\gamma_T=4,d=6,B=72$ anchor; sizes remain separate.

Independent train/validation/test streams start in $|0\cdots0\rangle$ with
128 washout steps and 480/160/320 rows. Independent diagnostic streams pair
32 length-six suffixes with four independently burned-in prefixes each;
shorter windows are nested tails. Newest symbols are sampled without
replacement, older coordinates independently. Seed namespaces are disjoint
across roots and cohorts.

\emph{Fitting and decision rules.} Training alone standardizes features.
Validation selects the affine ridge penalty
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \lambda\in\{&0,10^{-10},10^{-8},10^{-6},10^{-4},10^{-2},\\
             &1,10^2,10^4,10^6,\infty\},
 \end{aligned}
\]
then train and validation data refit the readout using economy singular-value
decomposition (relative cutoff $10^{-6}$) for finite penalties;
$\lambda=\infty$ predicts only the intercept.

Linear delay, quadratic delay \cite{gauthier2021next}, dimension-matched random-tanh, intercept-only,
and reset--replay share windows and splits. Replay applies the last $d$ target
channels to a fixed state, refitting (retrained) or retaining (compiled) the
affine readout. Each cell selects its best nonconstant classical control.

The preregistered rule requires NRMSE $\leq0.10$ in at least 10 of 12
roots (one-sided probability $0.0193$ under fair nonzero signs).
Only roots are independent units, not rows, observables, suffixes or shot
replicates. Confidence intervals use 20,000 paired root-block bootstraps.

After excluding variances below $10^{-8}$, diagnosis checks history-floor
failure, total-QELM success, then replay success. Representation limited
means history passes, the fitted QELM fails, and replay succeeds, not that
the best approximation using QELM features fails.

\subsubsection{Finite-Shot Extension}

For 12 four-qubit targets at $\gamma_T=4,d=6$, this exploratory study uses
$Q\in\{300,1200,4800,19200\}$ shots per input,
split equally in integer shares across snapshots and global-Pauli settings.
Joint multinomial bit-string draws yield compatible single-site expectations.
All 16 replicates independently resample train/validation/test features and
refit. Validation selects $\lambda$ per $B$, then $B$, without test targets.
Exact targets isolate emulator sampling; Section~\ref{sec:numerics}'s
intermediate-feature study instead uses exact expectations throughout.

\subsubsection{Long-Context Extension}

The prospective cohort uses 12 new roots, $N_T=5$ (45 outputs),
$\gamma_T\in\{1,4\}$, and $d\in\{2,4,6,8,10\}$.
A fixed ten-qubit QELM encodes one input per qubit, with nested checkpoints
giving $B\in\{30,60,120\}$. Panels pair 32 length-ten suffixes with four
prefixes each; other settings are inherited and all cells retained.
Paired history contrasts use 10,000 root-block bootstrap draws.
The QELM--quadratic comparison fixes $B=120$ before testing.
Cross-cohort changes include register size, not just context.

\subsubsection{Time-Series Demonstrations}
\label{app:laser-demonstration}

Both studies share four-qubit random $XX$ dynamics, field 0.5,
$\Delta t=0.5$, local loss, and 48 exact Pauli expectations at four checkpoints.
The QELM encodes four inputs without subsequent drive.
Fitting uses training-only standardization, an
unpenalized intercept, and validation-selected
$\lambda\in\{10^{-8},10^{-6},10^{-4},10^{-2},1,100\}$.

\emph{Laser-driven emulation.} Figure~\ref{fig:long-context}(a,c) uses
12 roots and consecutive 4,096/1,024/2,048-sample
train/validation/test blocks of the Santa Fe A continuation.
Training min/max scaling, clipping and 33-level rounding are shared.
Each split resets, then discards 128 steps. At loss 4 or 0.2, only the
QELM readout is fitted, targeting the QRC's final $Z_0$ expectation.
The first root's first 128 scored rows are shown; labels use all 1,920 test
rows. Two 128-input test prefixes share a 128-input continuation;
Figure~\ref{fig:long-context}(c) shows its first 48 inputs.
After four shared inputs, half the QRC gap bounds the larger pairwise
absolute error, not global $D_d/2$ or NRMSE.

\emph{Additional NARMA10 prediction.}
\label{app:task-forecasting}
Twenty-four fresh roots replicate an earlier 12-root study without pooling.
Independent uniform $u_t\in[0,1]$ streams per root
and split use the same lengths and washout. The recurrence uses $0.2u_t$
\cite{fujii2017harnessing}; both readouts learn unquantized $y_{t+1}$
from rounded inputs through $t$, never past targets. Each model selects
loss from $\{0.1,0.3,1,4\}$ by validation. Replay fixes the QRC dynamics
and refits each readout; longer windows cost more evolution steps.
Quadratic controls use 14/four-input and 90/twelve-input features.

NRMSE uses training-target standard deviation. NARMA gates require at least
20/24 positive roots and a positive 95\% mean lower bound from 10,000 paired
root bootstraps (no simultaneous coverage). Hardware errors and backaction
are omitted. Code link withheld for anonymity.
