Existing work supplies the main ingredients but not their observable-level
connection.  QRCs use persistent dynamics as a temporal resource across spin,
noisy-processor, oscillator, and quantum-input settings
\cite{nakajima2019spatial,chen2020temporal,
govia2021oscillator,kalfus2022hilbert}.
Fading-memory and echo-state results explain when old state dependence becomes
negligible \cite{boyd1985fading,grigoryeva2018sas,grigoryeva2018esn,
grigoryeva2019differentiable,gonon2021fading,martinezpena2023finite,
martinezpena2025input,kobayashi2024esp,kobayashi2026coherence}, while
dissipation, feedback, monitoring, and non-Markovianity alter that horizon
\cite{sannia2024dissipation,kobayashi2024feedback,morguisancho2026monitored,
sannia2026nonmarkovian,paparelle2026memory}.  Reset-window and
replay schemes encode finite delays explicitly
\cite{kawanabe2026tdqelm,assil2026memory,mujal2023measurement,
cindrak2024restriction}; universality results establish rich functions of the
supplied window \cite{goto2021universal,yu2024nonasymptotic}.  Transfer,
Krylov, task-coverage, conditional-information, and Fisher-mode analyses
describe dynamically accessible directions
\cite{gross2026ptm,cindrak2025krylov,domingo2026diagnosing,keenan2026storage,
wang2026fisher,morguisancho2026monitored}.  Separate finite-sampling studies
quantify which encoded directions can be resolved
\cite{hu2023sampling,ahmed2025finite}.  Classical realization theory
characterizes minimal switched models through reachability and observability
\cite{petreczky2013realization}.  Joint-spectral-radius theory treats uniform
matrix-product stability \cite{rota1960joint,jungers2009joint,
philippe2016constrained}, and protected sectors retain symmetry-protected state
components \cite{zanardi1997noiseless,albert2014symmetries}.
What remains unresolved by these separate results is one architecture-level
boundary: how visible recurrent information sets the optimum finite-window
prediction error, and when a growing reset-QELM can close it.  We establish that
boundary through the exact scalar minimax law $D_d/2$, an if-and-only-if
emulation theorem, and a reachable-observable quotient whose common-product
joint spectral radius gives the exact root-decay rate; a protected mode realizes
the complementary obstruction.
