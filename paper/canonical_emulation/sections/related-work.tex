Existing research provides several foundations for this comparison.
Fading-memory theory studies systems whose dependence on the distant past
becomes progressively weaker \cite{boyd1985fading,grigoryeva2018sas}.
Quantum reservoir studies establish conditions for forgetting earlier states
\cite{martinezpena2023finite,sannia2024dissipation}, while replay and window methods make recent
history explicitly available to reset models
\cite{mujal2023measurement,cindrak2024restriction,kawanabe2026tdqelm}. Universal approximation
results address which functions quantum feature models can represent
\cite{goto2021universal,yu2024nonasymptotic}. Our analysis connects these
questions by identifying exactly which missing historical information limits
finite-window emulation.
