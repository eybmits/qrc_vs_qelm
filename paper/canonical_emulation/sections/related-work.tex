\par Recurrent quantum reservoirs use persistent dynamics as a temporal
resource, with realizations in spin networks, engineered-memory and noisy
processors, nonlinear oscillators, Rydberg arrays, Gaussian systems, and
quantum-state inputs
\cite{nakajima2019spatial,kutvonen2020optimizing,chen2020temporal,
govia2021oscillator,kalfus2022hilbert,bravo2022rydberg,
nokkala2021gaussian}.  Fading-memory theory explains when
old-state dependence contracts enough for finite-window approximation
\cite{boyd1985fading,grigoryeva2018sas,grigoryeva2018esn,
grigoryeva2019differentiable,gonon2021fading,
martinezpena2023finite,martinezpena2025input,kobayashi2024esp,
kobayashi2026coherence}.  Dissipation, feedback, monitored evolution, and
non-Markovian environments move that horizon
\cite{sannia2024dissipation,kobayashi2024feedback,
morguisancho2026monitored,sannia2026nonmarkovian,paparelle2026memory,
ricci2026damping}.  Retained memory still leaves an interface question: whether
its output-visible component is determined by the learner's supplied suffix.

Reset-window and delayed-input learners encode recent history explicitly.
Parallel time-delayed QELMs upload a finite delay vector, memory-enhanced QELMs
add explicit delays, and rewinding or artificial memory restriction resets a
recurrent QRC before replaying a suffix
\cite{kawanabe2026tdqelm,assil2026memory,mujal2023measurement,
cindrak2024restriction}.  Quantum feature maps already have universal
approximation constructions for continuous finite-dimensional functions; the
parallel repeated-encoding construction includes the multivariate Bernstein
basis used here and admits quantitative circuit bounds
\cite{goto2021universal,yu2024nonasymptotic}.  The present bound combines that
known representation certificate with QRC history truncation and finite-shot
estimation in one observable-level resource law.  Pauli-transfer,
Krylov, task-coverage, conditional-information, and Fisher-mode analyses
describe which functions of that suffix become accessible
\cite{gross2026ptm,cindrak2025krylov,domingo2026diagnosing,keenan2026storage,
wang2026fisher}.  Finite sampling and measurement choice then determine which
directions are resolved
\cite{hu2023sampling,kora2026noise,ahmed2025finite,mujal2023measurement,
morguisancho2026monitored,huang2020shadows}.  A quadratic
nonlinear-vector-autoregression model supplies the strongest classical delay
control in the numerical study \cite{gauthier2021next}.  These methods process
the supplied suffix; they cannot distinguish two admissible histories with the
same supplied suffix.

Protected quantum modes provide the physical mechanism.
Decoherence-free subspaces and symmetry-protected steady sectors retain
state components that symmetric dynamics cannot erase
\cite{zanardi1997noiseless,lidar1998dfs,albert2014symmetries}.  The two-qubit
construction connects such a protected component to an addressable input and a
declared Pauli output, making it protected, excitable, and visible.  Previous
work studies memory capacity and finite-delay architectures; this work asks
whether the visible delay window is sufficient for the declared target in the
first place.
